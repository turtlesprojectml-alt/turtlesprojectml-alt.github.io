Overview

At a glance

What it is

400 high-fidelity simulations of 3D turbulent wakes behind cylinders of varying shape, Reynolds number and angle of attack, each with 400 time steps on an irregular mesh.

Why it’s hard

Real 3D turbulence with an active energy cascade, millions of irregularly placed points per frame, and long time horizons, all in the same benchmark.

What’s included

A fixed 320/40/40 split, mini-TurtLES subsets from 0.8 TB, a 5 GB debug case, and per-case velocity spectra at wake probes.

Mid-span slices of the same simulation as the 3D view above. The dataset stores the full 3D fields; slices are for visualisation only.

What existing benchmarks miss

Many “dynamic fluid” benchmarks are unsteady or chaotic without containing 3D turbulence, where vortex stretching drives energy from large eddies down to small ones. Others reach 3D and industrial scale but only at steady state, or stay on coarse point clouds. TurtLES covers all of these at once.

Dataset

400 turbulent wakes across a four-parameter design space

Each case places a super-elliptic cylinder in a uniform inflow. In this range the wake transitions to turbulence while the boundary layer stays laminar, so every trajectory contains an active 3D energy cascade.

Parameters

Shape n
diamond, ellipse, rounded rectangle, rectangle (n = 1, 2, 5, ∞), 100 cases each
Half-length a/D
1/6 to 3/2, log-uniform; thickness fixed at D = 1
Reynolds Re
1000 to 3900
Angle α
0° to 45°

A Sobol’ sequence spreads the 400 samples evenly over the parameter space, without clusters or gaps.

Simulation

Solver
High-order Flux Reconstruction implicit LES (PyFR), Mach 0.2
Mesh
Automated Gmsh mesh, extruded into 30 span-wise layers over 2πD
Validation
Matches DNS and experiments for circular, square and 5:1 rectangular cylinders at Re 1000–3900

What’s in a case

Fields
pressure p, density ρ, velocity u, v, w
Points
2.9–10.5 M fixed nodes per case
Snapshots
400, every Δt = 0.04732 D/U, after the start-up transient
Region
x/D ∈ [−3, 10], y/D ∈ [−3.5, 3.5], z/D ∈ [0, 2π]
Format
HDF5: one coordinate file plus four chunks of 100 frames
Probe spectra
u and v spectra at 2.5D, 3.5D, 4.5D behind the object, with script

Get the data

Splits and smaller subsets

Trajectories come pre-split into 320 training, 40 validation and 40 test cases. Each shape family is split exactly 80/10/10, and every subset covers the same range of Re, α and a as the full dataset.

Pick a mini-TurtLES subset

Fixed, named subsets keep results comparable across labs: drop shape families, keep fewer snapshots, or both.

Scope

Limitations

One regime

Turbulent cylinder wakes in the shear-layer transition range. Other geometries and Reynolds numbers are not covered.

Solver reference

Ground truth is implicit LES, not DNS or experiment.

Finite window

Snapshots are sub-sampled in time, and the window is too short for converged statistics such as Reynolds stresses.
